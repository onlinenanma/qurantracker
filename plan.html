Structured Memorisation & Teaching Framework

Consistent revision and structured daily targets are essential for long-term Quran retention (Hifz). This page provides structured plans for independent learners alongside practical coaching strategies for parents and teachers.

📅 General Memorisation Plans

Beginner Plan

Focuses on short surahs (Juz Amma). Memorize 3 to 5 lines or 1 short verse per day with high repetition using the app audio tool.

Intermediate Plan

Target half a page to 1 full page per day. Dedicate 60% of study time to new memorization and 40% to reviewing past lessons.

Advanced / Intensive

Aim for 1 to 2 pages daily utilizing the daily page range (Wird) tool. Strict weekly consolidation loops to prevent forgetting.

👥 Guide for Teachers & Parents

1. Establish a Routine

Practice at the same time every day—ideally early in the morning after Fajr when the mind is fresh and free from distractions.

2. Use Chunking & Repetition

Break long verses into small phrases. Use the app's repetition counter set to 5 or 10 repeats per phrase before linking them together.

3. Listen Before Reading

Have the student listen to expert reciters (like Al-Husary or Alafasy) 3 times to absorb correct Makharij (pronunciation) and Tajweed rules before attempting recitation.

4. Daily & Weekly Revision (Muraja'ah)

Never skip revision. A good student spends more time revising old portions than memorizing new ones. Log daily sessions using the progress tracking table.
