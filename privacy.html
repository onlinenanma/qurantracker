Privacy Policy

Quran Learning App (Quranlearning.app)

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Summary: Quranlearning.app does not collect, store, share, or track any personal data or personally identifiable information. All user progress and preferences stay strictly on your local device.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Quranlearning.app does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, device identifiers, or IP addresses.
  • No User Accounts: You can use all features of the application without creating an account or providing any personal details.
  • Local Storage Only: To provide progress tracking (e.g., selected Surahs, repetition targets, display modes, and saved session logs), the app uses standard local browser storage (localStorage). This data remains 100% on your personal device, is never uploaded to our servers, and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser cache or clicking "Clear History" inside the app.

2. Audio and External Resource Requests

To deliver Quranic audio recitations and Arabic text script, Quranlearning.app fetches public Islamic media resources directly from third-party APIs (such as Al Quran Cloud and EveryAyah).

These network requests are standard technical fetches required to stream audio and render text on your screen. No user data, personal details, or tracking identifiers are sent during these requests.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Because we do not collect or hold any user data, we do not sell, rent, share, or trade any information with third parties, including advertisers, data brokers, analytics services, or government entities.

4. Tracking and Cookies

Quranlearning.app does not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party tracking pixels to monitor user activity or build advertising profiles. Standard offline caching technologies (Service Workers) are used exclusively to enable Progressive Web App (PWA) functionality and fast loading on your device.

5. Third-Party Sharing Functions

The application includes optional share buttons allowing you to share your progress text via your device's native sharing dialogs (such as WhatsApp, Email, or Clipboard). If you choose to use these options, the data is handled directly by the external apps installed on your device, according to their respective privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Quranlearning.app is safe for users of all ages, including children learning the Quran. Because we strictly collect zero personal information from any user, we comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR, and global privacy laws.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time as we enhance the app's features. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
