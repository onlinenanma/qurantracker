Welcome to Quran Learning App

This guide explains how to configure settings, control audio recitation, utilize repetition targets, and navigate verse and Mus'haf viewing modes effectively.

⚙️ Settings & Configuration Panel

Tap the Select button on the main panel to open the sliding configuration menu. Here you can change the Surah, pick specific verse boundaries (From/To), modify the repetition target count, select your preferred reciter, or load daily page ranges (Wird).

🔊 Audio Controls & Playback

  • Play / Stop All (▶ / ⏹): Starts continuous recitation of your selected verses from beginning to end based on your repetition targets.
  • Pause / Resume (⏸ / ▶): Temporarily pauses current playback or resumes right where you left off.
  • Individual Verse Listen Button: Tap the play icon on any specific verse card to listen to or loop that individual verse.

🔁 Verse Cards & Repetition Targets

Each verse card tracks your progress dynamically. Tapping the repeat badge allows you to configure individual repetition goals. Once your target counts are completed, you can save your session progress directly to your local weekly log.

📜 Viewing Modes (Verses vs. Mus'haf Page)

Toggle between standard Verse mode (detailed translation breakdown with interactive audio chips) and Mus'haf Page mode (real high-resolution page view mirroring physical Mushaf layouts) using the header toggle button or the settings panel.
